The game controls:



For Ti-89 and Ti-89T:

In-Game;

	Arrows:
		Left:	Turn left/Walk to the left
		Right:	Turn Right/Walk to the Right
		Up:	Enter pipes which points down etc.
		Down:	Duck when mario is large, Enter pipes etc.

	2nd:
		Jump/Fly/Swim

	Diamond:
		Run
		Throw fireball

	Esc:
		Pause/Mid-game menu

	+/- :
		Adjust contrast









In map:
	

	Arrows:
		Left:	Walk to the left
		Right:	Walk to the Right
		Up:	Walk up
		Down:	Walk down

	2nd:
		Enter a level in map / Use current item from list

	Diamond:
		Toggle statusbar (status mode or itemlist mode)

	Esc:
		Pause/Mid-game menu

	+/- :
		Adjust contrast





In menus:

	Enter:
		Choose the current alternative

	Esc:
		Back/Exit

	Up/Down:
		Move cursor

How to

	Fly:	To fly you need to have the racoon suit or the p-wing.
		You can fly when all the triangles in the statusbar have turned dark
		and the P is flashing. To make this happend you need to run continously for some time.
		Then, when the P is flashing, press and hold the jump button and you will fly until 
		you release the button.
		When you have the P-wing you can fly at any time, but it will only last for one level.
		

	Swim:	Whem you are in water you move left and right as usual, by using the arrow keys. 
		Mowement in water is generally mush slower than on land, unless you swim.
		You can swim by using the Jump key, and it will also bring you upwards a little.
		You will slowly sink when not swimming. To sink faster, simply press the "Down" arrow key.

	Get out of Quicksand:
		Pressing the Jump key!

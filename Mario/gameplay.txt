Super Mario 68K features a gameplay similar to all other mario games:

You stomp on enemies, find powerups, collect coins and have a lot of fun while completing 
level after level!


You must reach the end of each level to get further in the game. You will meet 
a lot of different creatures that has been cursed by bowser. They will do anything to stop you! 
To defeat them you can stomp on most of them, but others you'll have to defeat in other ways.
You can also shoot fireballs at them if you have found the fire flower ;-)

You will find helpful powerups like the flower, racoon suit, extra lives etc.

Your ultimate goal is to rescue princess Toadstool, who was kidnapped by Bowser. She is kept inside 
Bowser's castle and you will have to go through a big bounch of worlds to reach there. 
On your way you will meet a lot of challenges in a lot of different environments. 



Your quest will take place through hostile deserts filled with quicksand, pyramids and hidden treasures.
You will have to dive down to the bottom of the deep oceans of the mushrom kingdom.

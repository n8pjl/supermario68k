





This is Super Mario 68K, a grayscale Mario game for your calculator! 
It features 8 worlds with maps, a lot of exiting levels and many hours of fun gameplay.









Explore and find your way through a lot of exiting worlds! You will meet many kind of challenges on your way.
When Bowser took over the Mushrom kingdom he sent out a lot of different troops, creatures like goombas, turtles, the Koopa Kiddos 
and even the Hammer Brothers! 
You will have to make your way through the fruitfull lands of the mushrom kingdom, 
through hostile deserts with pyramids and oasis, and if you're lucky you might find hidden treasures!

You will have to cross deep oceans with plenty of different creatures, and if you're good at diving you might find sunken ships from Bowsers navy!












The game also supports external, custom-made levels! 
With this feature you will never get tired of the game ;-)

With the leveleditor you can make your own levels, worldmaps and even a brand new Mario game!
